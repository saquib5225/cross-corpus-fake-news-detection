# Dissertation Writing Plan

| Chapter | Purpose and subsections | Evidence, tables and figures | Key argument / weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Introduction | Problem; motivation; aim; objectives; chapter roadmap. | `FINAL_RESEARCH_AUDIT.md`; evidence map. | High: high internal scores alone are insufficient evidence of cross-corpus robustness. |
| 2. Literature Review | Fake-news classification; dataset shift; leakage/independence; classical vs transformer methods. | Add verified scholarly citations only; do not use repository material as literature evidence. | High: locate the study’s methodological gap. |
| 3. Methodology | Research design; datasets; validation; cleaning; splits; models; metrics; testing; reproducibility/ethics. | Dataset master, methodology master, overlap audit, Stage 2A/2B reports. | Highest: make the independence decision and selection protocol auditable. |
| 4. Experimental Results | Dataset audit; ISOT metrics; FakeNewsAMT metrics; gaps; tests; error analysis. | Final master tables; Figures 01–03, 05; statistical table; selected Stage 4 outputs. | Highest: report facts before interpretation. |
| 5. Discussion | Generalisation, model comparison, possible shift interpretation, contribution, limits, practical implications. | Findings, limitations, contribution and error reports. | High: distinguish observed evidence from possible interpretation. |
| 6. Conclusion and Future Work | Answer RQs; contribution; limitations; next studies. | Evidence map and final story. | Medium: concise, no new claims. |

The original 10-part guide may be used if the programme requires it; this six-chapter plan is a writing recommendation, not a replacement for university rules. Allocate most words to Chapters 3–5. Use appendices for full hyperparameters, hashes, detailed statistics, confusion matrices and integrity records.
